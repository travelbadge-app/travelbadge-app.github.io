Privacy policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

The short version

TravelBadge keeps your travels on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we don't collect, sell or share your personal information.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • The places you've stamped or added to your bucket list, with visit dates and counts.
  • Photos you add to a visit. The app keeps its own copy inside the app, and nothing is uploaded.
  • Your name, username and picture, if you choose to set them, and your settings.
  • Your friend code, a random code the app makes for you. It isn't connected to who you are.

Deleting the app deletes all of this from your phone. You can also erase your progress any time in Settings.

Photos

The app looks at your photos only when you choose some to add to a visit, using the iPhone's own photo picker, which shows the app only the photos you pick. If you save your passport card, the app asks for permission to add that one image to your photos. It can't see the rest of your library.

Backups you make

"Share a backup" in Settings creates a block of text with your places, dates and profile, and hands it to the iPhone's share sheet. You decide where it goes, or whether it goes anywhere. We never receive it. A backup doesn't include your photos.

Maps

The Map tab can show Apple Maps. Apple provides those maps, and Apple's own privacy policy covers that part. TravelBadge doesn't use your location, and doesn't ask for it.

Purchases

TravelBadge Plus is a subscription. Apple handles the payment and the sign-up, and we never see your card or Apple ID details.

Children

TravelBadge isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when optional accounts arrive, the date above changes and we'll explain what's different.
