Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version

TravelBadge keeps your travels on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we don't sell or share your personal information. The only information about you that leaves your phone is an anonymous ID and your subscription status, sent to our subscription service when you use TravelBadge Plus.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • The places you've stamped or added to your bucket list, with visit dates and counts.
  • Photos you add to a visit. The app keeps its own copy inside the app, and nothing is uploaded.
  • Your name, username and picture, if you choose to set them, and your settings.
  • Your friend code, a random code the app makes for you. It isn't connected to who you are.

Deleting the app deletes all of this from your phone. You can also erase your progress any time in Settings.

Photos

When you add photos to a visit, you choose them in the iPhone's own photo picker, which shows the app only the photos you pick. If you save your passport card, the app asks for permission to add that one image to your photos.

"Find places from my photos" is optional, and it only runs if you start it and give permission. It reads where and when the photos in your library were taken, to suggest the countries and US states you have been to. This happens on your iPhone: no photo, location or date is uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else. The app keeps only the places you choose to stamp and, if you stamp them from Settings, the month of your first photo there. You can give access to only some photos, and you can turn access off any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Home screen widgets

If you add a TravelBadge widget, the app hands it your counts, level and a picture of your map. This stays on your iPhone, in storage shared only between the app and its widgets.

Backups you make

"Share a backup" in Settings creates a block of text with your places, dates and profile, and hands it to the iPhone's share sheet. You decide where it goes, or whether it goes anywhere. We never receive it. A backup doesn't include your photos.

Maps

The Map tab can show Apple Maps. Apple provides those maps, and Apple's own privacy policy covers that part. TravelBadge doesn't use your location, and doesn't ask for it.

Purchases

TravelBadge Plus is a subscription. Apple handles the payment and the sign-up, and we never see your card or Apple ID details.

To tell whether Plus is on, the app uses a subscription service called RevenueCat. It receives a random anonymous ID the app makes, your purchase status and what you bought, and your device's basic details such as app version and country. It isn't connected to your name, your travels or your photos, and it isn't used for ads or tracking. Your places, dates and photos are never sent to it.

App updates

The app checks for small updates when it opens, using a service from Expo. Like any internet request, that check includes your device's IP address and basic details such as the app version. It contains nothing about you or your travels.

Children

TravelBadge isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when optional accounts arrive, the date above changes and we'll explain what's different.

Contact

TravelBadge is made by an independent developer. Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: travelbadgesupport@gmail.com
