Common questions

Where are my travels saved?

On your iPhone only. They're normally included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or your computer). For an extra copy you control, open Profile, then Settings, then Share a backup and save the text somewhere safe, like Notes. To bring it back, use Restore from a backup and paste it in. Photos aren't part of a backup.

How do I delete my data?

Use Reset progress in Settings, or delete the app. We hold nothing on any server, so there's nothing else to delete.

How do I manage or cancel TravelBadge Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. If you've subscribed before and don't see Plus, tap Restore purchases on the Plus screen in the app.

Can I use TravelBadge with VoiceOver?

Yes. The maps are drawings, so use the List view on the Map tab to browse and stamp every place. Text follows your iPhone's text size, and animations follow Reduce Motion.
